# MiniWay Application – End‑User Manual

---

## 📖 Overview

**MiniWay** is a React Native ride‑sharing app that connects **Conductors** (trip managers) with **Drivers**. This manual walks you through the complete user journey – from logging in, creating and managing a trip, to successfully completing it.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1️⃣ Prerequisites
- **iOS / Android device** with the **Expo Go** app installed (or an emulator).
- **Internet connection** (Wi‑Fi or mobile data).
- A **MiniWay account** (provided by your organization). If you don’t have one, contact your admin.

### 2️⃣ Install the App
1. Open the QR code generated by `npm run dev` in the project’s root (or download the published build from your internal app store).
2. Scan the QR code with **Expo Go** or install the APK/IPA directly.

### 3️⃣ Launch the App
- Tap the MiniWay icon. You’ll be greeted with the **Login** screen.

---

## 🔐 Login Flow

1. **Enter Credentials** – Input your **email** and **password**.
2. **Tap “Sign In”**.
3. The app validates the credentials against Supabase.
4. **Single‑Session Protection** – If the same account is already active on another device, you’ll see a **“Session Expired”** alert and be logged out.
5. Upon success, you land on the **Dashboard** appropriate for your role (Conductor or Driver).

> **Tip:** Enable push notifications in your device settings to receive real‑time trip updates.

---

## 👤 Conductor Workflow (Trip Manager)

### 1️⃣ Dashboard Overview
- **Active Trips** – List of ongoing trips with status badges.
- **Create New Trip** – Button at the top‑right corner.

### 2️⃣ Creating a Trip
1. Tap **“+ New Trip”.**
2. **Select Destination** – The map opens; tap the desired drop‑off point.
3. **Set Capacity** – Choose the maximum number of passengers.
4. **Confirm** – Press **“Create Trip”**.
5. The new trip appears in **Active Trips** with a unique **Trip ID**.

### 3️⃣ Managing Passengers
- **Add Guest** – Tap the **“Add Guest”** button, fill in name and optional notes, then **Save**.
- **Passenger Cards** – Each passenger shows:
  - Name
  - Status (Waiting / On‑board)
  - **Drop** button (swipe left) to remove a passenger.
- **Refresh List** – Pull‑to‑refresh the passenger list without reloading the whole screen.

### 4️⃣ Real‑Time Tracking
- The map displays the driver’s live location (updated via Supabase realtime).
- **Status Indicators** – Green dot = driver on‑route, red = offline.

### 5️⃣ Completing the Trip
1. When the driver reaches the final destination, the **“Finish Trip”** button becomes active.
2. Tap **“Finish Trip”**.
3. A confirmation modal shows trip statistics (duration, distance, passenger count).
4. Confirm to close the trip. The trip moves to **Past Trips** and analytics are logged.

---

## 🚗 Driver Workflow

### 1️⃣ Dashboard Overview
- **Available Trips** – List of trips you can accept.
- **Current Trip** – Shows the trip you’re currently handling.

### 2️⃣ Accepting a Trip
1. Tap **“Accept”** on a desired trip.
2. The trip moves to **Current Trip** and the map centers on the pickup location.

### 3️⃣ Navigation & Live Broadcast
- **Start Navigation** – Press **“Start”** to begin broadcasting location.
- The app sends GPS updates every **5 seconds** to Supabase.
- The Conductor sees your location in real time.

### 4️⃣ Updating Trip Status
- **Pick‑up** – When you pick up passengers, tap **“On‑board”**.
- **Arrive** – Upon reaching the destination, tap **“Arrive”** to complete the trip.
- The app logs **GPS accuracy** (average, best, worst) for analytics.

## 📊 Post‑Trip Analytics (Visible to Admins)
- **Crash Count** – Daily crash statistics.
- **GPS Accuracy** – Detailed per‑trip metrics.
- **QR Scan Success Rate** – For any QR‑based check‑ins.

## 🛠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Unable to log in** | Verify email/password, ensure `.env` Supabase keys are correct, and that your device has internet access. |
| **Location not updating** | Grant location permissions, enable GPS, and ensure a stable network connection. |
| **Passenger list not refreshing** | Pull‑to‑refresh or restart the app. |
| **Session expired unexpectedly** | Another device logged in with the same account; you’ll be logged out automatically. |
| **Trip does not finish** | Ensure you have tapped **“Arrive”**; if the button is disabled, check that the driver’s location has reached the destination radius (≈30 m). |

## 📦 Building & Deploying (For Power Users)
```bash
# Development server
npm run dev

# Production bundle
npm run build
npx expo export --output-dir ./dist
```
Deploy the `dist` folder to your chosen hosting platform (Vercel, Netlify, etc.).
