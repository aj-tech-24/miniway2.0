                           1. BRAND OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Miniway is a modern public transportation app designed to connect commuters
with bus drivers efficiently. The brand identity focuses on:

• Trust & Reliability - Users need to feel confident in the service
• Modernity & Innovation - The app should feel contemporary and tech-forward
• Clarity & Ease - Navigation and information should be immediately clear
• Safety & Professionalism - Both drivers and passengers need assurance

================================================================================
                     7. ACCESSIBILITY CONSIDERATIONS
================================================================================

CONTRAST RATIOS:
• Primary text (#1E293B) on white: 12.6:1 ✓ (AAA compliant)
• Primary cyan (#0891B2) on white: 4.5:1 ✓ (AA compliant)
• White text on cyan gradient: 4.5:1+ ✓ (AA compliant)
• Red error text (#EF4444) on white: 4.0:1 ✓ (AA for large text)

COLOR BLINDNESS CONSIDERATIONS:
• Semantic colors are paired with icons/shapes for redundancy
• Status badges include both color and text labels
• Location markers use both color and icon differentiation
• Error states combine red color with icon indicators

BEST PRACTICES APPLIED:
• Never rely solely on color to convey information
• Use sufficient contrast for all text
• Provide visual indicators beyond color (icons, shapes, patterns)
• Test with color blindness simulators
